About PrimeCool Tech

Built to keep Lagos comfortable, one system at a time.

PrimeCool Tech Ltd is a Lagos-based HVAC company handling the maintenance, repair, installation and supply of air conditioning systems for homes and businesses. The company was formed around a simple goal: give Lagos a team it can actually rely on for reliable, high-quality cooling — without the runaround.

Our engineers work across residential and commercial jobs, from a single split unit in a bedroom to multi-unit setups for offices and institutions, always aiming for comfort and energy efficiency that lasts beyond the first service call.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

We recommend a full maintenance visit every 4–6 months for most homes and businesses in Lagos, given the dust and near-constant use. Units running heavily (offices, shops) may benefit from quarterly checks.

Warm or weak airflow, unusual noises, water leaking from the indoor unit, bad odours, and a spike in your electricity bill are all signs it's time to get a technician to look at your system.

Yes — we run a 24/7 emergency line for urgent breakdowns, so you're never stuck without a technician even outside normal working hours.

Keep filters clean, service the unit regularly, avoid setting the thermostat too low, seal up the room properly, and have the outdoor unit's coils checked so it isn't working harder than it needs to.
